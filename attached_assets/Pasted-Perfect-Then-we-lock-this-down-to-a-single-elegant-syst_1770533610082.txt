# **Travony — Minimal, Perfectly Wired**

## One Core Idea (Everything Derives From This)

> **Travony is where people post and talk *while they are moving through a place*.**

---

## 1. One Object Only: **The Move**

There is **only one content type** in Travony.

### A Move is:

* a short photo or video
* one caption
* created only while moving
* automatically tied to:

  * place (zone)
  * time
  * movement type

There are no stories, posts, tweets, snaps.
Everything is a **Move**.

---

## 2. Profiles = Collection of Moves

Profiles exist, but they are simple.

A profile shows:

* avatar + name
* **Move Trail** (vertical journey feed)

No likes count.
No reposts.
No highlights.

Your identity is **where you’ve moved**.

---

## 3. Discovery = Place First, People Second

There is **no global home feed**.

Discovery happens in only two ways:

1. **Map → Zone**
2. **Search → Place**

You find Moves because:

* you’re nearby
* or you care about that place

Not because someone is famous.

---

## 4. Talking Is Always Attached to a Move or Zone

There is **no free chat**.

### Two ways to talk:

1. **Zone Chat** – people currently there
2. **Move Reply** – temporary thread under a Move

If movement context disappears → chat disappears.

This keeps conversations natural and safe.

---

## 5. Saving = Pinning to Your Trail

Saving a Move:

* pins it to your personal trail
* optionally bookmarks the zone

Saved Moves are private by default.

No public collections.
No boards.

---

## 6. Following Exists but Is Silent

You can follow someone, but:

* you only see their Moves **when they are moving**
* or when they post in a zone you enter

No feed farming.
No pressure.

---

## 7. Ride Booking Is Just Another Move Trigger

Booking a ride:

* unlocks longer Move limits
* improves trust
* enhances visibility

But visually:

* it’s still just movement

This keeps the product unified.

---

## 8. What This Replaces (Cleanly)

* X → replaced by **Move captions**
* Instagram → replaced by **Move visuals**
* Snapchat → replaced by **Move ephemerality**
* Google Maps → replaced by **human movement signal**

All with **one object**.

---

## 9. Absolute MVP (Cannot Be Smaller)

1. Move creation (movement-gated)
2. Move Trail profile
3. Map Zones → Moves
4. Zone Chat
5. Move Replies
6. Save to Trail

That’s it.
