# First: Reframe the Relationship

Right now your structure likely implies:

Driver ↔ Rider
Supply ↔ Demand
Job ↔ Service

Vehicle Owners ↔ Mobility Clients
Asset Network ↔ Access Layer

Same mechanics.

# The Connecting-Dots Insight

Ride-hailing apps (like Uber) positioned themselves as:

You don’t need to compete there.

> “Privately owned vehicles powering a smart mobility network.”

This reframes both sides as participants in something intelligent.
